# 📅 When do Australian tech internships and grad programs open?

Most Australian early-career hiring happens **a year ahead** and in **one big wave early in the year**.
If you only remember one thing: **have your resume ready by February.**

> Dates move every year and differ by employer. Use this as a rough guide and always check the employer's site.
> ⭐ Star the [main list](README.md) to see roles as they open.

## The yearly cycle

| Month | What's usually happening |
|---|---|
| **Jan** | Quiet. Polish your resume, LinkedIn and GitHub. Some early graduate expressions of interest open. |
| **Feb** | 🔥 **Peak season starts.** Most summer internship (vacationer) and graduate programs open. |
| **Mar** | 🔥 Peak. Banks, telcos, Big 4 and government programs are mostly open. Online tests and video interviews start. |
| **Apr** | 🔥 Many programs close. Assessment centres and interviews. |
| **May** | Last few closings. Offers start going out. |
| **Jun–Jul** | Winter break. Some winter internships run. Global tech and quant firms start opening roles for the next cycle. |
| **Aug–Sep** | Quant and trading firms, and some global tech companies, open internships and grad roles for the following year. Some second-round programs. |
| **Oct** | Smaller employers and start-ups hire, often with short notice. Final offers for most programs. |
| **Nov–Feb** | Summer internships run. Graduates who started the previous year finish their first rotation. |

## What that means by program

| Program | Who it's for | Usually starts | Applications usually open | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Summer internship / vacationer** | Penultimate-year students | Late Nov to Feb | Feb to Apr of the same year | CommBank's 2026/27 summer intern program runs 23 Nov 2026 to 29 Jan 2027. Atlassian's 2026/27 Australian internships run Nov 2026 to Feb 2027, and one of its intern ads closed on 26 Apr 2026. |
| **Graduate program** | Final-year students and recent grads | Feb of the following year | Feb to Apr, a year before you start | NAB usually advertises grad roles in Feb and Mar. nbn's 2027 graduate recruitment opened in March 2026. |
| **Quant / trading internships and grad roles** | Strong maths, CS and engineering students | Varies | Often Jul to Sep, earlier than everyone else | Optiver, IMC, Jane Street, SIG, Tower Research |
| **Government grad programs and cadetships** | Usually Australian citizens | Feb | Usually the first half of the year | APS graduate programs, ASD, Defence. Check [apsjobs.gov.au](https://www.apsjobs.gov.au). |
| **Winter internships** | All years, fewer spots | Jun to Jul | Usually autumn (Mar to May) | Mostly start-ups and some consultancies |

## Tips

- **Apply early.** Many programs review applications as they arrive and close early when they have enough.
- **Check eligibility first.** Many programs need Australian or NZ citizenship or permanent residency. Some, like a few big-tech internships, also take international students.
- **"Expression of interest" ads count.** Registering means you get emailed the day applications open.
- **Look at last year's closed roles** in the [list](README.md#closed). Companies tend to hire at the same time each year.
